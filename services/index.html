Scope, stated plainly

What home care is — and is not.

Families often arrive at this decision after a hospital stay, a fall, or a diagnosis. Knowing which kind of help applies makes the first call much easier.

Non-medical home care

  • Bathing, dressing, and grooming
  • Meals, hydration, and light housekeeping
  • Companionship and errands
  • Medication reminders
  • Mobility awareness around the home

Physician-ordered clinical care

Skilled nursing, therapy, and wound care are separate services ordered by a medical provider. Call the office at 301-326-2225 to discuss whether a request falls inside our service scope.

Not sure which service fits?

Describe the situation and we will help identify the best starting point, the schedule we can support, and what happens next.
