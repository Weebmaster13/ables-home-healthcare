Questions

Answers before the first call.

If your question is not here, call 301-326-2225 or send a message.

What kind of care does Ables Home Healthcare provide?

Non-medical daily living support: personal care such as bathing and dressing, companion care, respite for family caregivers, dementia and Alzheimer's support, post-hospital routine help, and live-in care. Skilled nursing, therapy, and wound care are physician-ordered clinical services and are outside our published service descriptions.

How do families request care?

Call the office at 301-326-2225, send a care request through this site, or submit a referral if you are a provider. A client or family member can also create a secure portal account to track requests and appointments.

What information helps before the first call?

The service address, who needs care and what changed, the daily routine, mobility or supervision concerns, weekly hours you are hoping for, the payment method, and an emergency contact. Having these ready usually shortens the process.

How much does care cost?

Cost depends on the type of service, the schedule, visit length, frequency, and caregiver availability. Request a consultation and the office will explain what applies to your situation. We do not publish a price list because it would not be accurate for every case.

Which payment options do you accept?

Community First Choice (CFC) and the Home and Community Based Options Waiver (HCBOW), Community Personal Assistance Services (CPAS), private pay by cash or card, and private insurance. Eligibility and coverage must be verified before care begins.

Is this website for emergencies?

No. This site is for non-emergency care coordination and general information, not medical advice. For urgent symptoms or immediate danger, call 911.

Does Medicare cover non-medical home care?

Medicare generally does not cover non-medical daily living support. Physician-ordered clinical home health is a different service category from non-medical home care, so it is worth asking the office which applies to you.

How are caregivers screened?

Caregiver applicants are reviewed for experience, references, and certification, and must consent to a background check. We are also required to verify credentials such as CNA, LPN, or RN licensure where a role depends on them.

What happens after a request or referral is submitted?

The office reviews the details, follows up to discuss the routine and schedule, outlines a care plan, and matches a caregiver when one is available. You are told what can realistically be supported before care starts.

What if the caregiver is not the right fit?

Contact the office with feedback. The care plan, schedule, or caregiver match can be reviewed when needs or preferences change.

Where do you provide care?

We accept care inquiries across the Maryland counties and Baltimore City listed on our home page. Availability still depends on the specific address, schedule, and care needs, so call to confirm.

Do you hire caregivers?

Yes. We hire CNAs, LPNs, and RNs. Certified applicants may need a current Maryland license or certification, CPR and first aid, a recent physical or TB test, and a background check. Apply through the caregiver application page.

Still have a question?

Call the office or send a care request and we will explain the options.
