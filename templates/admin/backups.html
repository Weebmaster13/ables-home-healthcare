Launch checklist

Backup policy before real client use

  • Confirm Render PostgreSQL automated backups or scheduled snapshots are enabled.
  • Create a test backup and confirm an admin can download it.
  • Decide where uploaded documents will be stored long term, such as Render disk, S3, Backblaze B2, or another approved storage service.
  • Write down who checks backups weekly and how long old backups are kept.
  • Do not store backup files on a public computer or share them through personal email.
