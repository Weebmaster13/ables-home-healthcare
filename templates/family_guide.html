Care questions

Questions families can ask during consultation.

  • Which daily routines are hardest to manage right now?
  • Does the client need hands-on help, reminders, supervision, or companionship?
  • What days and times would reduce the most family stress?
  • Are there fall risks, memory concerns, or transportation needs?
  • How should the office communicate visit updates or schedule changes?
