Privacy

Privacy Policy

How Ables Home Healthcare collects, uses, and protects website information.

This policy is starter website copy and must be reviewed by the company owner and a qualified healthcare privacy or legal professional before collecting real client health information.

Information We Collect

This website may collect contact details, service inquiry details, referral information, appointment request information, caregiver application information, uploaded documents, communication records, and account details submitted through forms or portals.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, coordinate requested services, review referrals, manage appointments, communicate with clients and caregivers, and improve care operations.

Care and Health Information

Home care inquiries can include sensitive personal or health-related information. Ables Home Healthcare should treat this information as confidential and use appropriate safeguards, limited access, secure hosting, strong passwords, audit logs, backups, staff training, and careful office procedures before using this website with real client information.

HIPAA and Compliance Review

If the company stores, transmits, or receives protected health information through this website, HIPAA and Maryland privacy requirements may apply. Before public launch, confirm whether hosting, email, document uploads, database backups, staff access, and any third-party services require business associate agreements or additional privacy controls.

Security

The application is prepared for HTTPS hosting, secure session cookies in production, role-based access, database backups, and administrator review. Final hosting, SSL, email configuration, and privacy compliance must be completed before live use.

Document Uploads

Portal document uploads may contain sensitive information. Users should upload only documents needed for care coordination, office review, eligibility, billing, or service administration. Uploaded documents are not for emergencies and should be reviewed only by authorized staff.

Email and SMS Reminders

If configured, the system may send appointment reminders by email or text message. Standard carrier messaging rates may apply. Families and caregivers should contact the office directly for schedule changes, urgent needs, or emergency concerns.

Retention and Backups

Information may be retained for business, care coordination, billing, legal, security, and compliance purposes. Backup copies may remain for a limited time even after active records are archived or deleted. The company should approve a written retention schedule before launch.

Professional Review

This page is professional starter copy, not legal advice. HIPAA, Maryland privacy requirements, consent language, record retention, and business associate obligations should be reviewed by a qualified healthcare attorney or compliance professional.
