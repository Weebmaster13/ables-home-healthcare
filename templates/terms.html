Terms of Use

Website terms for visitors, clients, caregivers, and referral partners.

Website Use

This website is provided for service information, appointment requests, referrals, account access, caregiver scheduling, and communication with Ables Home Healthcare.

No Emergency Services

This website is not for emergencies or urgent medical needs. In an emergency, call 911 or contact a qualified healthcare provider immediately.

Service Information

Website descriptions are for general information. Actual services, schedules, caregiver availability, eligibility, and care plans may vary based on client needs and agency review.

User Accounts

Users are responsible for protecting login details and using the portal only for lawful, authorized purposes. Administrators may update roles, review appointments, and manage users.

Portal Documents and Messages

Users should submit only accurate information and documents relevant to care coordination or office administration. Do not upload emergency requests, unrelated files, or information you are not authorized to share.

Reminder Communications

Email or text reminders are convenience notices only. They do not replace direct office confirmation, emergency services, or professional advice.

Professional Review

These terms are starter copy for a business website and should be reviewed by a qualified attorney before production use.
