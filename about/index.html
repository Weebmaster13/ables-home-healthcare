About

A local agency with a clear scope of care.

Ables Home Healthcare is a residential service agency based in Silver Spring, Maryland. We help people remain at home with dependable non-medical support.

How we work

Home care usually begins during a difficult week — after a hospital stay, a fall, a new diagnosis, or a season when a family caregiver simply needs help. Our first job is to make that moment easier to navigate.

Care is planned around the client's own daily routine rather than a generic checklist. Families are told plainly which services we provide, which belong with a medical provider, and what schedule we can realistically support.

What families can expect

  • Caregivers who are screened and reviewed before entering a client's home.
  • A care plan covering tasks, schedule, preferences, and emergency contacts.
  • One office contact for scheduling questions and changes.
  • Feedback welcomed when the plan, the schedule, or the caregiver fit needs review.
Licensing

Licensed to operate in Maryland.

Residential Service Agency

Ables Home Healthcare operates as an RSA licensed by the Maryland Department of Health. Maryland license number R3114.

Service scope

Our published services are non-medical: personal care, companionship, respite, and daily living support. Licensing, assessment, and scheduling determine what can be provided for a specific client.
