Services listed in the brochure

Daily living and companionship

  • Companionship, geriatric care
  • Hospice care, respite care
  • Light housekeeping
  • Range of motion exercise
  • Accompany to doctor appointments
  • Running errands and grocery shopping

Personal care

  • Alzheimer care, dementia care
  • Bathing and grooming
  • Lifting and transferring
  • Light meal preparation and feeding
  • Personal laundry and linen change
  • Medication reminders

Clinical and assessment services

  • IV therapy and injections
  • Wound care and dressing changes
  • Catheter care and changes
  • Tube feeding
  • Tracheotomy and ventilation care
  • Initial and follow-up assessments
  • Home environment evaluation
  • Family training and behaviour consultation

The clinical services listed in the printed brochure are provided only when licensing and a medical order allow. They are separate from the non-medical services described elsewhere on this website. Call the office to confirm whether a specific service applies to your situation.
