Privacy Policy

How Ables Home Healthcare handles information submitted through this website.

ⓘ

Review pending: this policy is being finalised with a qualified healthcare privacy professional before the site collects real client health information. Contact the office with any privacy question.

Information we collect

This website may collect contact details, service inquiry and referral information, appointment requests, caregiver application information, uploaded documents, communication records, and portal account details submitted through its forms.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, coordinate requested services, review referrals, manage appointments, communicate with clients and caregivers, and improve care operations.

Care and health information

Home care inquiries can include sensitive personal or health-related information. We treat this information as confidential and apply appropriate safeguards, limited access, and careful office procedures.

HIPAA and Maryland requirements

Where the company stores, transmits, or receives protected health information through this website, HIPAA and Maryland privacy requirements may apply. Hosting, email, document uploads, database backups, staff access, and third-party services are being reviewed against those requirements before launch.

Security

The application is prepared for HTTPS hosting, secure session cookies in production, role-based access, database backups, and administrator review.

Document uploads

Portal document uploads may contain sensitive information. Upload only documents needed for care coordination, office review, eligibility, billing, or service administration. Uploaded documents are not monitored for emergencies and are reviewed only by authorized staff.

Email and text reminders

If configured, the system may send appointment reminders by email or text message. Standard carrier messaging rates may apply. Contact the office directly for schedule changes, urgent needs, or emergency concerns.

Retention and backups

Information may be retained for business, care coordination, billing, legal, security, and compliance purposes. Backup copies may remain for a limited time after active records are archived or deleted. A written retention schedule is being finalised.

This page is not legal advice. HIPAA, Maryland privacy requirements, consent language, record retention, and business associate obligations should be confirmed by a qualified healthcare attorney or compliance professional.
